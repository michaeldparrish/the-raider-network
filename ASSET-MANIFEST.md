# The Raider Network v5 — Asset Manifest

Generated by `tools/make_manifest.py` (crops by `tools/crop_assets.py`). **156 assets** cut from 10 concept boards, plus 3 banners carried over from v4.

**All artwork is AI-generated concept art for visual identity only.** Text and descriptions baked into the boards (item blurbs, coordinates, stats such as "2,847 Active Raiders") were cropped out and are **not** used as game data. Item art is labelled on the site as "Concept art · not in-game appearance".
