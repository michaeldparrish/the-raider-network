# The Raider Network v6.4 — Amplification Guide & Amplification Hunt

Branch: `feature/v6.4-amplification`, based on the v6.3 release. The working copy was verified identical to `main` @ `fabb831`: all 882 files and tree `0be4e70f…` match.

Nothing in this work was pushed, previewed on Cloudflare, migrated remotely or deployed. Since the multi-material update (section 6), v6.4 includes one additive migration, `0005_trail_plans.sql`. It was tested only on disposable local databases.

## 1. What's new

**Amplification Guide — `/amplification`**

An illustrated field guide, pre-rendered as HTML so it reads and indexes without JavaScript. It has a sticky section navigation and these sections:

- **Overview:**
  - what amplification is, and the five unlock prerequisites
  - the three steps: activate, research & upgrade, repair
  - how upgrade branches and restrictions work
- **Modules & weapons:**
  - Mk. I–V shown with the gold module art, growing in size from tier to tier
  - each tier lists its supported weapons, linked to their catalog pages
- **Farming locations:** ten visually distinct sections:
  - ARC Frigate
  - Fallen Emperor
  - Emperor Payloads & Redirection
  - Pendola Pass
  - Electromagnetic Storm
  - ARC Assessor (Close Scrutiny)
  - The Queen
  - Matriarch
  - ARC Turbine
  - Recycling amplified gear

  Each section shows what it is, how to access it and its loot, with the materials it gives as clickable tiles.
- **All 15 materials:** a searchable, filterable library using the approved artwork. Each card opens a detail view with:
  - image
  - rarity, category, sell value, weight, stack size and recycling, each with its verification status
  - where to find it
  - every weapon, upgrade and research step that uses it, with quantities
  - notes
  - actions: Start a Loot Hunt, Add to Loot Trail, Loot Intel entry

  Detail views have direct links, for example `/amplification#item-assessor-matrix`.
- **Upgrade requirements:** for all 15 weapons, 44 upgrades with materials and quantities, plus what each upgrade requires and excludes. Each weapon has a "Plan a hunt" link.
- **Verification:** open questions and conflicts, plus sources with the date they were checked.
- **Labels:** every fact is marked **Confirmed**, **Community-reported**, **Unverified** or **Conflicting**. No drop rates are shown, because no source publishes them.

**Amplification Hunt — `/amplification/hunt`**

A weapon-first mission planner:

1. **Choose a weapon:** the 15 weapons, grouped by module tier, with the existing weapon images.
2. **Choose upgrades:**
   - Each upgrade shows its effect text and cost.
   - Prerequisites are added automatically, for example Semi-Auto when you pick Bettina X-Rounds.
   - Incompatible branches are disabled and the reason is shown.
3. **Materials checklist:** the module, the upgrade materials and the research parts.
   - Totals are added up, so shared prerequisites are never double-counted.
   - A material needed for both an upgrade and research is shown once with the combined total (for example Equalizer Queen Reactor: 3 + 2 = 5).
   - Each line shows its verification status.
4. **Where to hunt:** every material, with its locations and verification, plus links to the Guide, a Loot Hunt and a Loot Trail.
5. **Start hunting:**
   - **Start Loot Hunt** opens the existing Quick Hunt form with the material filled in.
   - **Add to Loot Trail** opens the existing Loot Trails dashboard. You pick a trail or create one, and the discovery form opens with the material filled in and linked.

The plan is kept in the URL, for example `?weapon=bettina&upgrade=x-rounds`. You can also start from a material with `?material=<id>`.

**Navigation**

- Main menu:
  - **Loot Hunts** has a dropdown with Loot Hunts and Amplification Hunt (orange, NEW).
  - It opens on hover, with the chevron button (click or touch), and with the keyboard (ArrowDown, Up/Down, Escape, Tab). Clicking outside closes it.
  - In the mobile menu, both entries are always shown.
- `/hunts`: the hero banner and all its functions are unchanged. The orange **Start a Loot Hunt** button and **My Loot Trails** (now with an icon) stay. A compact neon **Amplification Hunt · NEW** button sits immediately to the right of My Loot Trails.
- **Guide button:** an orange **AMPLIFICATION** link in the top-right secondary bar, which you chose (option 1). It also appears in the mobile menu's secondary links. The Hunt page links back to the Guide.
- Below 420 px the secondary bar hides its duplicate "Guides" link, which points to the same place as "Routes", so AMPLIFICATION fits. It is still in the mobile menu.

**Catalog**

- The 10 missing items are added through a new source file, `tools/source-data/amplification.json`, which `tools/build_data.py` merges in. They therefore survive rebuilds, and the build automatically stops adding them once the community snapshot includes them.
  - Amplification Module Mk. I–V
  - Pressure Regulator
  - Pneumatic Actuator
  - Fluted Rotor Core
  - Propellant Valve Assembly
  - Amplified Fragments

  - Fossilized Lightning

  - show an "amplification material" panel on their item page that links to the Guide and Hunt.

## 2. Verification of game information

I checked the facts on 2026-10-24 against these sources:
- the community wiki (Amplified Weapons and item pages)
- ArcRaidersHub
- timesaver.gg
- KeenGamer
- Boosting Factory
- allthings.how
- ARC Tracker
- MetaForge pages
- the 2.0 patch notes summary

Results:
- **Your cheat sheet matches the sources on 42 of 44 upgrade costs.**
- **Conflicts:** Burletta and Canto Sprint Shooting cost 4 Fluted Rotor Core on the wiki, versus 3 on the cheat sheet. They are flagged as Conflicting, and the Hunt totals use 4.
- **Other conflicts, all flagged on the Guide:**
  - **Module recycling:** spare modules recycle into 7 Amplified Fragments according to 4 sources, but the wiki says they can't be recycled.
  - **Research projects:** 8 upgrades have no research project in the game files, but an older wiki page names one for each.
  - **Sprint Shooting research:** the Mobile-Fire Grip Fittings research costs Skulker Drivers or Skulker Targeters depending on the source, and the quantity differs.
  - **Extra exclusions:** one guide lists extra exclusions for Rattler, Renegade and Burletta.
  - **Frigate maps:** sources disagree on whether the ARC Frigate appears on Riven Tides.
  - **Payload naming:** most sources say "Emperor Payload", some say "Harvester Payload".
  - **Recycling outputs:** Assessor Matrix and Fossilized Lightning recycling outputs differ between sources.
- **Unverified or single-source, labelled as such:**
  - Propellant Valve Assembly and Fluted Rotor Core locations (tracker data and one report each).
  - Turbine Compressor, which is reported from ARC Turbine remains. It is no longer "unknown", but it is still not confirmed.
  - Which maps can roll the Electromagnetic Storm.
  - Which part of the Matriarch drops its reactor.
  - Effect descriptions, which come from the wiki and guides, not the game client.
- **Location names:**
  - "Penbora Pass" is **Pendola Pass**.
  - The Fallen Emperor and Redirection are both on Pendola Pass.

## 6. Multi-material hunting plans (update)

**Hunt page flow**

1. **Select the weapon.**
2. **Already amplified?**
   - "No, I am starting amplification" adds the matching module (for example Mk. II for Burletta) as the activation step.
   - "Yes, my weapon is already amplified" leaves the module out.
3. **Choose enhancements.** Several compatible ones can be chosen. Prerequisites are added automatically, and exclusive branches are blocked with the reason shown.
4. **Review materials,** in three separate parts:
   - initial amplification (only if needed);
   - each selected enhancement, with its install and research materials;
   - combined materials, with Needed, **I have**, **Still to hunt** and the verification status.

   Every material has its own checkbox, plus **Select all** and **Select none**.
5. **Track the hunt.** **Start Hunt for Selected Materials (n)** offers two options:
   - **Track in a Loot Trail** (recommended). Pick one of your trails, or start a new one with a map for discovery pins.
   - **Post a Quick Hunt.** One board post lists the build and the selected materials, with only the quantities still to hunt.

   **Hunt a single material instead** keeps the earlier single-material option.

**Loot Trail plans (migration 0005, six new tables)**

| Table | Holds |
|---|---|
| `loot_trail_plans` | Weapon, catalog weapon, module, title, status and the data date |
| `loot_trail_plan_enhancements` | Each enhancement, marked chosen or prerequisite |
| `loot_trail_plan_requirements` | Quantities per stage: activation, enhancement or research |
| `loot_trail_plan_members` | Each Raider's activation: required, not needed or completed, with the completion date |
| `loot_trail_plan_installed` | Each Raider's installed enhancements, with dates |
| `loot_trail_plan_progress` | Each Raider's had, collected and hunting values per material |

How it behaves:
- **Quantities come from the server.** It recalculates every quantity from the published amplification data. Quantities sent by the browser are ignored, and exclusive combinations are rejected.
- **Each Raider tracks their own stage and counts.** Squad members can join the same build with their own "already amplified?" answer.
- **Completing a stage keeps history.** Marking activation or an enhancement complete keeps the collection history. Materials spent on an installed enhancement are not counted again for the next one, so nothing is counted twice.
- **The owner sees everyone; contributors see only themselves.** The owner sees each accepted member's stage and what they still need to hunt. Plans never appear on public trails.
- **Plans reuse the existing trail features.** **Record discovery** on any plan material opens the existing discovery form with that material filled in. Sessions, pins, screenshots and review are unchanged.

**Quick Hunts**

- Quick Hunts are still saved in the browser only. One post can now carry several materials and the build.
- The board card shows "Hunting · N materials" with each material, and links back to the saved plan.
- Filtering by any listed material finds the post, and each material's item page lists it.
- Older single-item hunts are unchanged.
