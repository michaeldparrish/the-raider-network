# The Raider Network v6.3 — Loot Trails: release report

Branch: `feature/v6.3-loot-trails` (local commits only). Nothing in this work was deployed, pushed or merged, and no remote D1 migrations were run. No remote R2 bucket was created or changed, and no production data or configuration was touched. All tests ran against throw-away local D1/R2 databases created by `tools/devserver.py`.

## 1. Feature summary

**Start a Loot Hunt popup** (`hunts.html`). The popup now offers two choices:
- **Quick Hunt** is the default. Its form (`#huntForm`) and its behaviour are unchanged from v6.2: it is only shown or hidden.
- **Loot Trail** swaps in a create form with a name, a map and an optional private description. Submitting it creates a private trail in D1 and opens it.

In local demo mode the Loot Trail option explains that it needs the server; Quick Hunts still work. You can link straight to the Loot Trail option with `hunts.html?new=&mode=trail`. The Loot Hunts banner also has a **My Loot Trails** link.

**Loot Trails dashboard** (`trails.html`):
- Squad invitations, with Accept and Decline buttons.
- **My Loot Trails** (owned) and **Joined trails**, each shown as a card with:
  - visibility, map and owner
  - counts of discoveries, approved discoveries, sessions and squad size
  - your own progress bar
- A **Start a Loot Trail** button.
- Loading, empty and error states.

**Trail page** (`trails.html?id=…`):
- **Header:** visibility and role chips, the private description, your own progress ("x of y"; rejected discoveries are not counted), a 5-step guide, and a notice after the trail is created.
- **Map:**
  - level tabs (Stella Montis has two levels) and zoom from 100% to 400%
  - numbered pins coloured by state: pending, approved, completed by me, rejected
  - "Show on map" jumps to the right level and highlights the pin
  - pins are positions on the map image, stored as 0–1 coordinates; there is no geolocation
  - uncalibrated maps (Riven Tides, Pendola Pass) say so; Pendola pins use the detailed map
- **Sessions:**
  - a list with title, notes, who started it, start and end times, and discovery count
  - a **Start session** form
  - an **End** button with a second click to confirm, available to the session's creator or the owner
  - ended sessions do not accept discoveries
- **Squad:**
  - owner first, then members with their role and status (invited, declined or removed)
  - the owner sees each accepted member's completion count; contributors only see accepted members and no counts
  - the owner can invite by username (`@` is optional) and withdraw or remove a member (with confirmation)
  - Raiders who declined or were removed can be re-invited
- **Visibility** (owner only): Private ⇄ Public, with a confirmation that says exactly what becomes visible.
- **Discoveries:**
  - filters for status, session, "recorded by me" and "not completed by me"
  - each card shows the session, the creator, the level and a linked Loot Intel item
  - notes, two private screenshot thumbnails, and a "Mark completed (just for me)" checkbox
  - owner actions: Approve, Reject, Back to pending, and Publish/Unpublish (with confirmation)
- **Record a discovery:**
  - pick the session (open sessions only) and the level, and enter a title (item suggestions link to Loot Intel) and notes
  - place a manual pin, which is required and can be moved
  - two **separate** screenshot controls (Map position, Loot), each with a local preview
  - client-side checks: PNG/JPEG/WebP only, 20 MB each, 40 MB combined
  - nothing uploads until Save
  - each upload shows progress; if one fails, the discovery is still saved and the message says to add the missing screenshot from its card
- **Screenshot replacement:** while a discovery is pending or rejected, its creator or the owner can choose **Remove to replace** (confirmed) and then **Add**. Approved discoveries are locked: there is no removal and no new upload until the owner sets the discovery back to pending.
- **Public view** (for non-members of a PUBLIC trail): the title, the map, and only discoveries that are APPROVED **and** published (title, item, notes and pin). It never shows the description, squad, sessions, screenshots or usernames.

**Design.** Uses the existing panels, chips, buttons and notice bars, with the same colours and type. It works on desktop and on phones down to 390 px. No new frameworks or libraries were added.

## 3. New endpoints (added to the received backend)

All of them require login. State-changing requests go through the existing same-origin check (`X-TRN-CSRF: 1` plus Origin). Identity always comes from the session cookie.

| Method | Path | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/me/trail-invitations` | signed-in Raider | Their own pending invitations: trail id, title, map, owner name and username, invited time. |
| POST | `/api/trails/:id/sessions/:sessionId/end` | session creator or trail owner | 409 if already ended; 404 for anyone else. |
| POST | `/api/trails/:id/members/remove` `{username}` | owner | Invited or accepted → REMOVED; the Raider can be re-invited later. |
| DELETE | `/api/trails/:id/discoveries/:discoveryId/images/:imageType` | owner or discovery creator | 409 when the discovery is APPROVED. Deletes the D1 row, then the R2 object. |

Extended existing endpoints:
- `GET /api/trails` now returns `owner` and `stats`.
- `GET /api/trails/:id` returns `view: MEMBER|PUBLIC`. The member view includes sessions, squad and permissions; the public view is minimal.
- `POST …/invitations` re-invites Raiders who declined or were removed.

Members and sessions are listed in the member view of `GET /api/trails/:id`, so there are no separate list endpoints.

## 5. Known issues and limitations

- **Pins are not game coordinates.** They are positions on the map image. Riven Tides and Pendola Pass are labelled uncalibrated.
- **Screenshots are never public**, even for published discoveries. This is by design and the page says so.
- **Republishing on a public toggle.** Making a trail private hides everything. Making it public again re-shows discoveries that are still marked published. The confirmation text explains this.
- **The owner can re-invite a Raider who declined** (rate-limited to 20 an hour). There is no "block this trail owner" option yet.
- **No archive or delete for trails yet.** The backend has `status` but no endpoint; archived trails show read-only.
- **No live updates.** Changes by squad mates appear after a reload or after any action that re-renders the page.
- **Pin and level are separate when recording.** If you move to another level after placing a pin, the pin keeps its original level. The status line names that level.

## 7. Local preview (http://localhost:8788)

```bash
cd ~/Downloads/the-raider-network-v5.3
npx wrangler d1 migrations apply raider-network-db --local    # applies 0003 + 0004 to your LOCAL database only
npx wrangler pages dev . --port 8788                          # local D1 + simulated R2 (TRAIL_IMAGES)
```

To walk through it by hand:
1. Register two Raiders, one in a normal window and one in a private window.
2. As Raider A, open **Loot Hunts → + Start a Loot Hunt → Loot Trail** and create a trail.
3. Invite Raider B. As B, accept the invitation from **Loot Trails**.
4. Start a session, record a discovery (pin plus two screenshots) and tick it as completed.
5. As A, approve the discovery, publish it, then make the trail public.
6. Open the trail link while logged out.

Automated tests:
```bash
python3 tools/trails_test.py && python3 tools/trails_ui_test.py
```
